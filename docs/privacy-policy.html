Clearway: Arrow Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Clearway: Arrow Puzzle ("the app") is published by Fahad Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and how you can delete it.

Information stored only on your device

These items stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your username, which you choose when you first open the app. It is not unique and is not uploaded.
  • Your coins, hints, undos, daily streak, language, sound and notification settings, and the board you are currently playing.
  • Feedback you have written but that has not been delivered yet (it waits on the device until you are online).

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account. The app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random anonymous user ID. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and the app does not collect your location.
  • Game progress sync. The app stores your progress in Google Cloud Firestore under your anonymous user ID so it can be restored: your highest level, stars per level, owned and selected arrow skins, claimed milestones, achievement progress, claimed achievements and daily missions.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from Settings, we store the text you write together with your anonymous user ID, the app version and the device platform (for example "android") in Cloud Firestore. Please do not put personal information in feedback.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics. We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and basic device information (such as device model and operating system version) so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Advertising. The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising where permitted. The app uses Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and, where it is required (for example in the EEA and UK), asks for your consent before ads are loaded. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules local notifications on your device (about an unfinished level and the daily challenge). They are created by the app on your device; we do not run a push-notification server and no data is sent for them. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run the game, restore your progress, read your feedback, fix crashes and show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob and the Google consent form: policies.google.com/privacy

Data is sent to these services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete your data in the app. Open Settings and choose Delete Account. This deletes your game progress from Cloud Firestore, the feedback reports linked to your anonymous user ID, and your anonymous Firebase account, and it resets the data stored on your device. It needs an internet connection; if it fails, try again later. Step-by-step instructions: delete-account.html.
  • Data held by Google. Crash reports (Crashlytics) and advertising data (AdMob) are handled by Google under its own policies and are not removed by Delete Account.
  • Advertising choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings, and you can change your consent choice where the consent form offers it.
  • If you no longer have the app. Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot tell which stored data belongs to you without your help. Contact us at the email below with your approximate install date and we will do what we can.
