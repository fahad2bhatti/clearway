Clearway: Arrow Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Clearway: Arrow Puzzle ("the app") is published by Fahad Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, stars, coins, hints, undos, settings) is stored locally on your device. This data stays on the device unless noted below.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account and progress sync. The app signs you in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. We store an anonymous user ID together with your highest level and star progress in Google Cloud Firestore so progress can be restored and the daily puzzle can be served. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Crash reports. We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and basic device information (such as device model and operating system version) to find and fix bugs.
  • Advertising. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising where permitted. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before ads are personalised. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run the game, sync progress, fix crashes and show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and data deletion

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings. To ask us to delete the progress data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the email below and tell us your approximate install date; because accounts are anonymous we may not be able to identify your record without your help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page.
