Clearway: Arrow Puzzle - Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Clearway: Arrow Puzzle is published by Fahad Apps. The app uses an anonymous account (a random ID, with no name, email or phone number). You can delete it, and the online data linked to it, from inside the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Make sure your phone is online.
  2. Open Clearway and go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the message and tap Confirm.

If the app says the deletion failed, check your connection and try again. It is safe to try again.

What is deleted

  • Your game progress stored online (highest level, stars, skins, milestones, achievements and daily missions).
  • The feedback reports you sent from the app.
  • Your anonymous account (the random user ID).
  • The data stored on your device by the app (progress, coins, hints, undos, settings and your username) is reset.

What is not deleted by the app

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and advertising data (Google AdMob) are handled by Google under its own policies. See our privacy policy for details and links.

If you no longer have the app

Deleting from inside the app is the only way we can be sure which data is yours, because accounts are anonymous. Reinstalling the app creates a new anonymous account, not the old one. If you uninstalled before deleting, email us at fahadsaeed2622@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Clearway data", your approximate install date, and, if you remember it, the text of any feedback you sent. We will try to find and delete matching data, but we may not be able to identify your record.
